Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers about train status, timetables and this site.

Frequently asked questions

Is the data real-time?

No. Timetables come from a community GTFS feed of the Indian Railways schedule (version 30 Aug 2026, unofficial) and can change at short notice. Live status, PNR and seats are available on the official NTES and IRCTC sites, which we link to.

How do I check live train running status?

Search the train on our Live Train Status page to get its route and schedule, then open the official NTES link for the live position and delay on the day of travel.

How do I check the time table of a train?

Enter the train number or name on the Train Schedule page. Every train also has its own page listing all halts, arrival and departure times and running days.

How do I find trains between two stations?

Use the Trains Between Stations tool, or open one of the popular route pages. Both list direct trains with departure, arrival, duration and running days.

How do I check PNR status?

PNR data is private booking data, so use the official PNR enquiry linked on our PNR Status page. That page also explains codes such as CNF, RAC, GNWL, RLWL and PQWL.

Where can I check seat availability?

On the official IRCTC site. Our Seat Availability page explains class codes and quotas and links to it.

How do I find my coach position?

Use the NTES coach composition enquiry or the coach indicator board at the station. See our Coach Position guide for what the codes mean.

How accurate are the running days and timings?

They are as accurate as the open feed. Railways revise timetables during the year and run special trains, so confirm on NTES before travel.

How does the language feature work?

On your first visit the page opens in your device language using browser translation. Tap the flashing orange button to go back to English, or pick any language from the header.

Do I need to create an account?

No. There is no sign-up and no login.

Can I install it as an app?

Yes. See the Get the App page for Android, iPhone and laptop steps.

Do you store my PNR or searches?

No. We never ask for your PNR and searches run in your browser against static data files.

Is this an official railway app?

No. It is an independent project and is not affiliated with any railway operator.

How often is the timetable updated?

Whenever a new version of the open GTFS feed is published and loaded into the site, all train, station and route pages are rebuilt.
