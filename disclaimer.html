Timetable data

Timetables come from the Indian Railways community GTFS feed (unofficial, version 30 Aug 2026) distributed through the Mobility Database. Timings, routes, halts, running days and train numbers may be incomplete or out of date. Only halting stations are included.

Verify before travel

Always confirm train timings, platforms and bookings with official railway sources such as NTES and IRCTC, and with station announcements.
