PNR Status Check

Check your booking and berth status on the official PNR enquiry, and decode every status code on your ticket.

A PNR (Passenger Name Record) is a private 10-digit booking number held by the railway reservation system, so it can be checked only on official channels. We never ask for or store your PNR.

Open official PNR enquiry

How to check PNR status

  1. Keep the 10-digit PNR from your ticket or booking SMS ready.
  2. Open the official enquiry with the button above.
  3. Enter the PNR and the captcha, then submit.
  4. Read the booking status, coach and berth, and the chart status.

PNR status codes explained

CodeMeaning
CNFConfirmed – coach and berth number are allotted.
RACReservation Against Cancellation – you get a seat to share and travel; a full berth is allotted if others cancel.
WLWaiting list – no berth yet; it moves up as confirmed tickets are cancelled.
GNWLGeneral Waiting List – the most common waiting list, with the best chance of confirmation.
RLWLRemote Location Waiting List – for stations away from the originating station.
PQWLPooled Quota Waiting List – for passengers boarding or leaving at intermediate stations that share a pooled quota.
RQWLRequest Waiting List – tickets from request-quota stations.
CKWLTatkal quota waiting list.
RSWLRoadside Station Waiting List.

Quota and waiting-list rules are set by Indian Railways and can change. The final authority is the railway reservation system.

Understanding chart preparation

The reservation chart is prepared a few hours before departure and decides which waiting-list and RAC tickets get a berth. Check your PNR again after the chart is prepared: tickets that are still waitlisted at that point are normally cancelled automatically and refunded, whereas RAC passengers can still travel.

Frequently asked questions

What is a PNR number?

PNR stands for Passenger Name Record. It is a 10-digit number printed on every reserved ticket that links all passengers on the booking to their journey, class and berth.

Do you store my PNR?

No. This page never asks for your PNR; it only links to the official enquiry.

When is the final chart prepared?

Charts are prepared a few hours before the train leaves its originating station. Check the official enquiry for the latest status.

What if my ticket is still waitlisted?

If a waitlisted e-ticket is not confirmed after chart preparation it is cancelled and the fare is refunded automatically. RAC tickets remain valid for travel.
