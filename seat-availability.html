Train Seat Availability

Find the right train here, then check live seat and berth availability on the official IRCTC site.

Seat availability is live reservation data held by the railway booking system, so it is shown only on the official site. Pick your train with trains between stations, then check availability for your date and class.

Open IRCTC train search

How to check seat availability

  1. Find the train number and the boarding station with the train search.
  2. Open IRCTC and enter the From and To station, the date and the quota.
  3. Press search and open the availability for each class (SL, 3A, 2A, 1A, CC and so on).
  4. Check the status for the dates around yours; a status such as AVAILABLE, RAC or WL shows your chance of a berth.

Quotas and booking window

  • GN – General quota. TQ – Tatkal. PT – Premium Tatkal. LD – Ladies. SS – Senior citizen / lower berth.
  • Advance reservation opens 60 days before the journey date (not counting the travel day) under current railway rules. Rules change, so confirm on IRCTC.
  • Waitlisted tickets are not guaranteed. See the PNR status codes to understand GNWL, RLWL and PQWL.

Frequently asked questions

How can I check seat availability for a train?

Open the official IRCTC site, enter your stations, date and quota, and read the availability shown for each class.

What does WL mean on a ticket?

WL means waiting list: no berth yet. It can still be confirmed if earlier passengers cancel. See the PNR page for details of GNWL, RLWL and PQWL.

What is the difference between General and Tatkal quota?

General quota opens for advance booking weeks ahead. Tatkal opens one day before the journey at a higher fare for last-minute travel.
