Trains Between Two Stations

Choose a start and end station to list every direct train, sorted by departure time, with duration and running days.

How to find trains between two stations

Type the first letters of a station name or its code (for example NDLS for New Delhi or HWH for Howrah), pick it from the list, do the same for the destination and press Search trains. Only trains that stop at both stations and visit the first before the second are listed.

Each result opens the full train page with every halt, arrival and departure time and the days of the week the train runs.

Reading the results

  • Dep / Arr – scheduled departure from your start station and arrival at your destination.
  • Duration – time between those two stops; +1d means you arrive the next day.
  • Runs – days of the week the train operates, starting from its origin.

For seat or berth availability on a train you have picked, use the seat availability guide.

Frequently asked questions

How do I find direct trains between two cities?

Use the search above with the station names or codes of the two cities. For cities with several stations (such as Delhi or Mumbai) try each station, or open the popular route pages listed below.

Does this show trains with a change of train?

No. Only direct trains that stop at both stations are shown.

Are the departure times live?

No, they are scheduled times from the timetable. Delays are published on the official NTES site.
