How to check train running status online

  1. Enter the train number or name in the search box.
  2. Open the result to see the route, halts, scheduled times and running days.
  3. On the day of travel, tap the official NTES link for the live position and delay.
  4. Use trains between stations, seat availability and PNR status to plan the rest of your journey.

Frequently asked questions

Is the running status real-time?

The timetable on this site comes from the open Indian Railways GTFS feed, so it shows scheduled times, routes and running days. Real-time delay and last-reported-station data is produced by the railway control system and published only on the official NTES site, which we link to on every train page.

How do I check my train time table?

Type the train number or name in the search box on this page, or open the complete train list and pick your train. You get every halt with arrival and departure times and the running days.

How do I find trains between two stations?

Use the Trains Between Stations tool or one of the popular route pages below; both list direct trains with duration and running days.

How do I check PNR status and seat availability?

Both are private reservation data that only the railway systems hold. Our PNR and seat pages explain every status code and link to the official enquiry.

Is Where Is My Train free?

Yes. It is free, needs no login and can be installed as an app on your phone or laptop.
