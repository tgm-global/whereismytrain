About Where Is My Train

An independent, free train information project.

Who we are

Where Is My Train is an independent train information project by TGM Global. It is not the official app of any railway, and it is not affiliated with any railway operator or ticketing company.

Our goal is simple: help travellers find the right train, platform and coach with a fast interface in the language they are comfortable with.

Where the data comes from

Timetable tools use the Indian Railways community GTFS feed (unofficial, version 30 Aug 2026) with 10,000+ trains, 8,500+ stations and running days, published through the Mobility Database. The feed is converted into small static files, so every search runs in your browser and nothing you type is sent to a server.

Because Indian Railways offers no free public live-data feed, live status, PNR, seats, coach position and platforms link to the official NTES and IRCTC sites.

Keeping the timetable fresh

When a newer feed is published the site data and every train, station and route page are rebuilt from it, so the schedule stays as current as the open data allows.
