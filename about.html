About Where Is My Train

Where Is My Train is an independent, open source train information project by TGM Global. It is not the official app of any railway, and it is not affiliated with any railway operator or ticketing company.

Our goal is simple: help travellers find the right train, platform and coach with a fast interface in the language they are comfortable with.

How it works

All pages are static and run in your browser. Timetable tools use a 2026 timetable of 10,000+ trains and 8,500+ stations from the Indian Railways community GTFS feed (unofficial, Aug 2026 version), including running days. Because Indian Railways offers no free public live-data feed, live status, PNR, seats, coach position and platforms link to the official NTES and IRCTC sites.
