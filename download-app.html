Get the Where Is My Train App

Install it in seconds. It opens full screen, works from your home screen and keeps the core pages available offline.

If the button is hidden, use the steps below for your device.

Install on your device

Android (Chrome)

  1. Open this site in Chrome.
  2. Tap the ⋮ menu.
  3. Choose Install app or Add to Home screen.

iPhone / iPad (Safari)

  1. Open this site in Safari.
  2. Tap the Share icon.
  3. Choose Add to Home Screen.

Laptop (Chrome / Edge)

  1. Click the install icon at the right of the address bar.
  2. Confirm Install.

What you get

  • Full-screen app experience
  • Faster repeat visits
  • Offline access to core pages
  • Automatic updates – no app store needed
  • Tiny download compared with a store app

Why a web app?

Where Is My Train is a progressive web app. It installs from your browser, uses very little storage, updates itself and works on Android, iPhone, Windows, Mac and Linux with the same link.

Frequently asked questions

Is the app free?

Yes. There is no charge, no account and no advertising-based sign-up.

Do I need the Play Store or App Store?

No. Install it directly from your browser using the steps above.

Does it work offline?

The core pages are cached after your first visit, so they open without a connection. Timetable searches need the data files to have been loaded once.
