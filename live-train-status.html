Live Train Running Status & Time Table

Type a train number or name to see its full route, halts, running days and scheduled times – then open the official NTES page for the live position.

How to check train running status

  1. Enter the 5-digit train number (for example 12951) or part of the train name in the box above.
  2. Pick the train from the suggestions and press Check train status.
  3. Read the scheduled arrival and departure at every halt, the day of journey and the days of the week the train runs.
  4. On the day of travel, open the NTES link on the result for the live position and delay.

What does train running status tell you?

A train running status answers three practical questions: where is the train now, how late is it, and when will it reach my station. Delays build up or shrink along the route because trains regain time at long non-stop stretches, so the expected arrival at your station matters more than the delay shown at the previous one.

This site shows the planned schedule for 10,000+ trains and 8,500+ stations. Live position and delay are available only from the railway’s own system, so we send you to the official site instead of showing numbers we cannot verify.

Search by train name, number or route

Not sure of the number? Type the train name, or use trains between stations to list every direct train from one station to another. Every train also has its own page with time table, stoppages and running days – browse them in the complete train list.

Frequently asked questions

Is the running status real-time?

The timetable on this site comes from the open Indian Railways GTFS feed, so it shows scheduled times, routes and running days. Real-time delay and last-reported-station data is produced by the railway control system and published only on the official NTES site, which we link to on every train page.

How do I find the train number?

Search by name in the box above, or use Trains Between Stations to list trains for your route. The 5-digit number is shown next to every train name.

Why is my train not found?

Check the number for typing errors. Special trains and very recent additions may not be in the timetable feed yet; the data is refreshed whenever a new feed is published.

Can I see the running days of a train?

Yes. Each result shows the days of the week the train runs, shown as M T W T F S S with active days highlighted.
