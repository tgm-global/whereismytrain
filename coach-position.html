Coach Position

Find your coach position on the platform using the official NTES.

Coach composition changes by rake and day, and is published live by the railway. Use NTES or the station display board to know where your coach stops.

Open NTES

Indian Railways has no free public data feed for this information, so we link to the official source instead of showing unverified results.
