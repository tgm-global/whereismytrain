Train Coach Position

Know where your coach stops on the platform before the train comes in.

Coach composition changes by rake and day and is published live by the railway. Use NTES or the coach display board on the platform to see exactly where your coach stops.

Open NTES

How to find coach position

  1. Open NTES and choose the train enquiry for coach composition (rake position).
  2. Enter the train number and, if asked, the boarding station.
  3. Read the coach order from the engine to the last coach and locate your coach number.
  4. At the station, check the coach indicator board on the platform – it lists the coach order for the next trains.

What the coach codes mean

CodeCoach type
S1, S2 …Sleeper class
B1, B2 …Third AC (3A)
A1, A2 …Second AC (2A)
H1First AC (1A)
C1, C2 …AC chair car
D1, D2 …Non-AC chair car
EOG / SLRGenerator or luggage-and-guard coach at the ends

Some trains use different codes; the order shown for your train on NTES is the one to follow.

Tips for boarding

  • Reach the platform early so you can walk to your coach before the train arrives.
  • The engine side is the front of the train; coach numbers normally increase away from it, but check the indicator board.
  • Rakes can be swapped at short notice, so re-check on the day of travel.

Frequently asked questions

How do I find my coach position?

Use the NTES coach composition enquiry for your train, or read the coach indicator board on the platform.

Is the coach position the same every day?

Not always. Rakes can be swapped or coaches added, so the composition can change on any day.

Where does the engine stop on the platform?

It depends on the station layout and the direction the train arrives from; check the indicator board.
