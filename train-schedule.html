Train Schedule & Time Table

Search a train to see its complete schedule: every stop with arrival, departure, day of journey and the days it runs.

What the schedule shows

For each train you get the origin and destination, total distance, the number of halts, and a stop-by-stop table with scheduled arrival, scheduled departure and the day of the journey (day 1, day 2…). The running-day strip shows which weekdays the train operates; days highlighted in blue are running days.

Tips for reading a time table

  • Times use the 24-hour clock, so 18:30 is 6:30 pm.
  • A “Day” value of 2 means the stop is on the second calendar day of the journey.
  • Running days are given for the day the train leaves its origin; at later stations the weekday may be one or more days after.

Frequently asked questions

How do I check the time table of a train?

Enter the train number or name above. The full stop-by-stop schedule opens with all arrival and departure times.

Which days does a train run?

The running-day strip shows the weekdays on which the train leaves its origin. Special trains also show the date until which the schedule is valid.

Is the running status real-time?

The timetable on this site comes from the open Indian Railways GTFS feed, so it shows scheduled times, routes and running days. Real-time delay and last-reported-station data is produced by the railway control system and published only on the official NTES site, which we link to on every train page.
