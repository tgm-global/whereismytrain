Train Platform Number

Find the platform for your train and avoid last-minute changes.

Platform numbers are assigned by the station control room on the day and can change at short notice. Check NTES or the station announcements for the latest platform.

Open NTES

How to find the platform

  1. Open NTES and choose the station enquiry for the station where you board.
  2. Look for your train in the list of arrivals and departures and read the platform column.
  3. Confirm on the large display boards at the station; they are updated as platforms change.
  4. Listen for the announcement before the train arrives.

Why platforms change

Platforms are allotted by the station controller depending on which platforms are free, other trains running late and track work. A train that normally uses platform 1 may therefore be sent to a different platform on a busy day, so re-check about 30 minutes before the scheduled arrival.

Before you go

  • Look up your train’s time on its schedule page and reach the station early.
  • Check the coach position so you can wait at the right end of the platform.

Frequently asked questions

How do I know the platform number of my train?

Use the NTES station enquiry, the display boards or the station announcements. Platform numbers are assigned on the day.

Can the platform change after it is announced?

Yes. Platform changes can happen at short notice, so keep checking until the train arrives.
