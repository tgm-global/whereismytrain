Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 November 2026

What we collect

We do not require accounts and we do not store the trains or stations you search. Searches run in your browser against static data files.

Language and cookies

We store your language choice in your browser (local storage) and use a translation cookie so pages open in your language. When a non-English language is active, the page loads Google Translate, which is subject to Google’s own privacy policy.

Hosting

This site is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may log standard technical data such as IP address for security and operations.

Your choices

Select English from the header or the orange button at any time. Clear your browser data to remove stored preferences.

Contact

Use the contact page for privacy questions.
